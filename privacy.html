Event Management Script: Privacy Policy

This is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It reads email in the owner's own Gmail account (kaieventsapp@gmail.com) to find Outlook calendar invitations and WorxHub notification emails, and uses them to manage events. The app accesses only the owner's own mailbox. It does not collect data from any other users, and it does not sell, share, or transfer email data to any third party. Data is processed only on the owner's own systems.
